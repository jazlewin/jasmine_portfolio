About

Jasmine Lewin is a reporter from Portland, Oregon, who is attending Northwestern University Medill School for a Master of Science degree in Journalism. Previously, Lewin worked as a radio reporter for KLCC in Eugene, Oregon, and then a newspaper reporter for The Astorian in Astoria, Oregon, focusing on criminal and civil proceedings. In her free time, she enjoys reading sci-fi novels and playing pickleball.

At Circuit Court, defendants with mental health challenges struggle to help with their cases

In Clatsop County, Oregon, a rising number of defendants are unable to assist in their own defense because of serious mental health conditions. Delays are worsening due to a shortage of treatment beds at the Oregon State Hospital and strict federal limits on how long they can be held there. The system lacks suitable in-county secure psychiatric facilities and community restoration options, leaving many in limbo after the court-ordered time ends without regaining legal competency.

Courts Reporter, The Astorian, May 17 2024.

Jury finds negligence, but no damages in lawsuit against Columbia Memorial Hospital

A Multnomah County jury found that Columbia Memorial Hospital was negligent during a 2017 childbirth but determined the negligence did not cause the child's severe permanent neurological injuries. The plaintiff had sought over $112 million in damages, alleging improper fetal monitoring led to brain injury.

Homeless campers evacuated after flooding in Seaside

Heavy rains struck Seaside, Oregon, flooding the city-sanctioned Mill Ponds homeless encampment and forcing the evacuation of people living in tents and tarps. City officials helped relocate the campers, highlighting the risks of establishing encampments in low-lying, flood-prone areas.
